OBJECTIVE:
MSP is the minimum price guaranteed by the Central Government for notified crops. Farmers should never sell below MSP. If market price falls below MSP, government agencies procure at MSP.

WHAT MSP MEANS FOR FARMER:
- Government guaranteed floor price for your crop
- If open market price falls below MSP: sell to government procurement agency
- Selling below MSP to private trader: report to district supply office
- MSP does not mean government will always buy: procurement depends on state scheme and storage availability

WHERE TO SELL AT MSP IN MAHARASHTRA:

For Cotton:
- CCI (Cotton Corporation of India): procurement centres in Vidarbha and Marathwada
- Contact: cotcorp.gov.in [VERIFY active procurement centres for 2025]
- Nearest CCI centre: check at District Supply Office or District Agriculture Office

For Soybean, Tur, Moong, Udid:
- NAFED (National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation) procurement [VERIFY active in Maharashtra 2025]
- State government procurement through Maharashtra State Agricultural Marketing Board (MSAMB)
- APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) yards: check if MSP procurement window is open

For Paddy:
- FCI (Food Corporation of India) procurement centres
- State government procurement through civil supplies corporation

For Wheat:
- FCI procurement in Maharashtra (limited compared to Punjab/Haryana)
- Check availability at District Supply Office

HOW MSP PROCUREMENT WORKS:
1. Farmer registers at APMC yard or procurement centre with: Aadhaar, bank account, land records
2. Brings crop meeting quality and moisture norms
3. Crop weighed and quality checked at centre
4. Payment within 48-72 hours to Aadhaar-linked bank account [VERIFY current timeline]
5. Farmer gets digital receipt

QUALITY NORMS FOR MSP PROCUREMENT:
- Moisture content: cotton 8-12% [VERIFY], soybean 12% max [VERIFY], wheat 12% max [VERIFY]
- Clean and free from foreign matter, pest damage, damaged grains above permissible limit
- Grading done at procurement centre
- Below quality rejected or bought at discount: know norms before arriving

WHEN MARKET PRICE IS BELOW MSP — WHAT TO DO:
1. First check if official MSP procurement is open in your district
2. If open: take crop to procurement centre with documents
3. If not open: hold crop if storage available
4. Contact District Supply Officer in writing about below-MSP market rates
5. Contact APMC secretary about activating price support
6. File complaint at krishi.maharashtra.gov.in if no action taken

PRICE MONITORING:
- Check daily APMC market prices: agmarknet.gov.in (national) [VERIFY]
- Maharashtra prices: msamb.com [VERIFY portal]
- Compare market price with MSP before selling

ONION AND TOMATO SPECIAL NOTE:
- Onion and Tomato are NOT under MSP
- Price crash is common: no government price guarantee
- NOPCO (National Cooperative Exports) and NAFED can procure onion in crash situations [VERIFY active status]
- Tomato: no procurement mechanism, highly volatile

- CCI procurement is the primary safety net for cotton farmers
- Cotton farmers in Vidarbha and Marathwada: do NOT sell below MSP without checking CCI procurement status
