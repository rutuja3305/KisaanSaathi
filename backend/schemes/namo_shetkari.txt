SCHEME NAME: Namo Shetkari Maha Samman Nidhi Yojana
SOURCE: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

---

OBJECTIVE:
Maharashtra state government's own farmer income support scheme — additional financial benefit on top of PM-KISAN to give farmers Rs. 12,000 per year total combined support.

---

BENEFIT:
- Rs. 6,000 [VERIFY] per year from Maharashtra state government
- Paid in 3 installments of Rs. 2,000 [VERIFY] each
- Combined with PM-KISAN: farmer receives Rs. 12,000 [VERIFY] per year total
- Paid via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Both PM-KISAN and Namo Shetkari installments often transferred together

---

ELIGIBILITY:
- All farmers eligible for PM-KISAN are automatically eligible
- Must be registered under PM-KISAN first — Namo Shetkari uses same database
- Land must be registered in farmer's name in Maharashtra land records
- No separate income or land size restriction beyond PM-KISAN criteria

---

CRITICAL POINT:
- Many eligible farmers are NOT receiving Namo Shetkari because their PM-KISAN registration has errors
- Fixing PM-KISAN registration errors automatically fixes Namo Shetkari eligibility
- If PM-KISAN is working but Namo Shetkari is not: check mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in separately

---

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Same as PM-KISAN: Aadhaar, 7/12 Utara, Aadhaar-linked bank account
- No additional documents required if PM-KISAN is already active

---

HOW TO APPLY:
- No separate application if already registered for PM-KISAN
- New registrations: mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in → Shetkari Yojana
- CSC (Common Service Centre) for assisted registration
- Talathi office for land record verification support

---

HOW TO CHECK STATUS:
- mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in → Beneficiary Status
- Enter Aadhaar number or application number
- If status shows "pending": land record issue or Aadhaar mismatch

---

COMMON ISSUES AND FIXES:
- Not registered: visit CSC with Aadhaar and 7/12 to register
- Registered but not receiving: check Aadhaar-bank linkage at bank branch
- Name mismatch: correction at Talathi office
- Land mutation not done: update Ferfar at Talathi office

---

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- Rs. 12,000 per year combined (PM-KISAN + Namo Shetkari) = significant regular income for small farmers
- Many distressed farmers are missing this entirely due to registration errors
- Recovering 2 years of missed payments = Rs. 24,000 potentially recoverable [VERIFY]
- This amount can meaningfully offset short-term loan repayment gaps

---

SEASON TIMING:
- Installments released same time as PM-KISAN (April-July, August-November, December-March)
- Check both PM-KISAN and Namo Shetkari status simultaneously
