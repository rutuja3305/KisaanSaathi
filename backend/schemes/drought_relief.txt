VERSION: Kharif 2025 / Rabi 2025-26

OBJECTIVE:
When drought is officially declared in Maharashtra districts, multiple relief measures are automatically activated for affected farmers.

HOW DROUGHT IS DECLARED IN MAHARASHTRA:
- State government assesses: rainfall deficit below 75% of normal AND crop loss above 33%
- Taluka-level survey by agriculture and revenue officials
- Tahsildar submits report to Collector
- Collector recommends to state government
- State cabinet approves drought declaration for specific talukas or districts
- Central team visits for confirmation of severe drought (for NDRF funds)

WHEN DROUGHT IS DECLARED — WHAT AUTOMATICALLY ACTIVATES:
1. KCC loan repayment moratorium (automatic for all crop loans in declared area)
2. Input subsidy disbursement begins
3. Employment guarantee scheme (MNREGA) work intensified
4. Water supply and fodder relief in affected talukas
5. Free seed distribution for next season in severely affected areas [VERIFY]

INPUT SUBSIDY (NDRF NORMS):
Rainfed crop areas:
- Crop loss 33% to 50%: Rs. 8,500 per hectare [VERIFY current NDRF norms]
- Crop loss above 50%: Rs. 17,000 per hectare [VERIFY]
- Maximum area covered: 2 hectares per farmer

Irrigated crop areas:
- Crop loss 33% to 50%: Rs. 17,000 per hectare [VERIFY]
- Crop loss above 50%: Rs. 34,000 per hectare [VERIFY]
- Maximum area covered: 2 hectares per farmer

Horticulture and annual crop areas (rainfed):
- Crop loss above 33%: Rs. 18,000 per hectare [VERIFY]
- Maximum area covered: 2 hectares per farmer

Note: SDRF (State Disaster Relief Fund) norms may provide additional amounts above NDRF [VERIFY Maharashtra SDRF rates]

HOW COMPENSATION REACHES FARMER:
- No separate application needed for input subsidy in drought-declared areas
- Based on village-level crop cutting survey by agriculture department
- Farmer's name must be in village records as cultivator
- Payment via DBT directly to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Timeline: typically 2-4 months after declaration [VERIFY]

FARMER ACTION WHEN DROUGHT DECLARED:
1. Do NOT wait passively: verify your name is in village cultivation records
2. Visit Talathi office: confirm your crop area and cultivation status is recorded correctly
3. If name missing from records: file application at Talathi immediately
4. Obtain crop failure certificate even if not required immediately (useful for bank restructuring)
5. Apply for KCC moratorium at bank with drought declaration order reference
6. Continue PMFBY claim process separately (drought compensation and PMFBY are independent)

RABI 2025-26 DROUGHT RISK:
- Rabi drought risk: lower than Kharif but exists in Solapur, Osmanabad, Latur talukas
- Irrigation dependency: Rabi crops need assured irrigation; dam storage levels critical
- Check dam storage levels: waterresources.maharashtra.gov.in [VERIFY portal]

EX-GRATIA FOR FARMER DEATHS:
- When farmer dies due to suicide linked to crop failure or debt: Rs. 1,00,000 ex-gratia [VERIFY current amount and conditions]
- Separate from Gopinath Munde accident insurance
- Family must apply at Collector office with documentation
- Criteria: confirmed farm distress link required [VERIFY eligibility criteria]

MNREGA DROUGHT EMPLOYMENT:
- In drought-declared areas: MNREGA work guaranteed within 5 km [VERIFY]
- Rate: Rs. 273 per day in Maharashtra [VERIFY current MNREGA rate]
- For farmers waiting for crop loss compensation: MNREGA provides immediate income
- Apply at Gram Panchayat with job card

FODDER RELIEF:
- In severe drought: government fodder camps opened for livestock
- Free or subsidized fodder provided
- Livestock is asset collateral for many farmers: protecting livestock is critical
- Village-level fodder distribution coordinated by Collectorate

WATER SUPPLY RELIEF:
- In declared drought talukas: tanker water supply to villages
- Farmers can request water for cattle through Gram Panchayat

DISTRICT COLLECTOR ROLE:
- Key decision-maker for all drought relief at district level
- Farmer grievances about relief: written application to Collector
- Collector office: all districts have dedicated grievance desk for farmer relief
