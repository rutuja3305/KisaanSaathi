SCENARIO 1: CROP FAILURE + KCC LOAN + NO PMFBY INSURANCE
Risk Level: CRITICAL
Profile: Farmer took KCC loan, crop failed this Kharif season, was not enrolled in PMFBY.

Why critical:
- No insurance means no claim to repay loan
- KCC repayment due after harvest but no harvest income
- Account risks becoming NPA within 3-6 months

Immediate actions (do in this order):
1. Do NOT ignore bank notices — approach bank branch immediately this week
2. Apply for KCC restructuring — bring crop failure certificate from Tahsildar office
3. Check if district is drought-declared — if yes, automatic moratorium may apply
4. Apply for Maharashtra drought input subsidy (Rs. 13,500 per hectare for rainfed crops) [VERIFY]
5. Check all pending PM-KISAN and Namo Shetkari installments — recover any unpaid amounts at CSC
6. For next season: enroll in PMFBY before Rabi deadline (31 December 2025) [VERIFY]

Documents to collect urgently:
- Crop failure certificate from Tahsildar office
- 7/12 Utara showing crop sown this season
- KCC loan account statement from bank
- Aadhaar and bank passbook

SCENARIO 2: CROP FAILURE + KCC LOAN + PMFBY ENROLLED
Risk Level: HIGH but manageable
Profile: Farmer has crop insurance but may not have filed claim correctly.

Why high but manageable:
- Insurance should cover loss but only if claim filed correctly within 72 hours
- Delay in reporting voids the claim entirely

Immediate actions:
1. Report crop loss within 72 hours via Crop Insurance App or call insurer — get acknowledgment number
2. Inform bank branch about crop failure and that insurance claim has been filed
3. Obtain crop failure certificate from Tahsildar as supporting document
4. Track claim status on pmfby.gov.in using policy number
5. If claim delayed beyond 60 days after harvest: escalate to District Agriculture Officer
6. Ensure bank does NOT mark account NPA while claim is pending — inform bank in writing

Critical timeline:
- 72 hours: Report crop loss (non-negotiable)
- 30 days: Submit all documents to insurance company
- 60 days after harvest: Expect claim settlement [VERIFY]

SCENARIO 3: FARMER HAS NO KCC, USING INFORMAL LOANS
Risk Level: HIGH
Profile: Small farmer using moneylender loans at high interest rates, not in formal credit system.

Why high risk:
- Informal loans at 24-36% vs KCC at 4% = debt trap
- No insurance means single crop failure causes multi-year debt

Immediate actions:
1. Apply for KCC at nearest bank or cooperative bank — even 0.5 hectare is eligible
2. Form or join a Joint Liability Group (JLG) if land ownership is an issue — 4-10 farmers together
3. Register for PM-KISAN immediately if not done — Rs. 6,000 per year income support
4. Register for Namo Shetkari — additional Rs. 6,000 per year from Maharashtra
5. Enroll in PMFBY as non-loanee farmer before next season deadline
6. Apply for Gopinath Munde accident insurance — completely free coverage

Key numbers:
- KCC at 4% on Rs. 1,00,000 = Rs. 4,000 interest per year
- Informal loan at 30% on Rs. 1,00,000 = Rs. 30,000 interest per year
- Difference = Rs. 26,000 saved per year by switching to KCC

SCENARIO 4: PM-KISAN NOT RECEIVED FOR SEVERAL MONTHS
Risk Level: MEDIUM
Profile: Farmer is eligible but has not received PM-KISAN installments due to registration errors.

Common causes and fixes:
- Aadhaar not linked to bank account → visit bank with Aadhaar card to link
- Name mismatch between Aadhaar and 7/12 → correction at Talathi office (takes 7-15 days)
- Land not mutated after father's death → update Ferfar at Talathi (takes 1-3 months)
- Duplicate registration → visit CSC with Aadhaar to resolve
- Bank account closed or frozen → activate or change account at bank

Steps to resolve:
1. Check status at pmkisan.gov.in → Beneficiary Status with Aadhaar number
2. Note the rejection reason shown on portal
3. Visit CSC with Aadhaar + 7/12 + bank passbook for correction
4. Talathi for land record corrections
5. District Agriculture Office if unresolved after 30 days

Potential recoverable amount:
- If missed 2 years PM-KISAN: Rs. 12,000 pending
- Including Namo Shetkari: Rs. 24,000 potentially recoverable [VERIFY]

SCENARIO 5: COTTON FARMER IN VIDARBHA OR MARATHWADA
Risk Level: VERY HIGH
Profile: Cotton farmers in Yavatmal, Amravati, Wardha, Nanded, Beed face compounded risks.

Why very high risk:
- Cotton input cost: Rs. 30,000-50,000 per acre [VERIFY] — seed, fertilizer, pesticide
- Cotton price volatile — MSP may not match market price
- Bt cotton pest resistance increasing — higher pesticide costs each year
- Region historically drought-prone — crop failure common

Specific checks for cotton farmers:
1. Verify cotton is notified under PMFBY in your district for Kharif 2025 — check pmfby.gov.in
2. Verify PMFBY enrollment at bank before Kharif deadline (31 July 2025) [VERIFY]
3. Check KCC limit is sufficient for actual input cost — request enhancement if needed
4. Register for PM-KISAN and Namo Shetkari if not done
5. Check Gopinath Munde accident insurance is active — free coverage
6. Check solar pump scheme eligibility to reduce diesel irrigation cost

MSP for Cotton 2025-26: [VERIFY at dfpd.gov.in before season]
- Medium staple cotton (F-414): Rs. 7,121+ per quintal [VERIFY]
- Long staple cotton (V-797): Rs. 7,521+ per quintal [VERIFY]
- Sell at or above MSP — report below-MSP offers to District Supply Office

SCENARIO 6: TENANT FARMER OR SHARECROPPER
Risk Level: HIGH
Profile: Farmer cultivates land they do not own — limited access to formal schemes.

Scheme eligibility summary:
- PM-KISAN: NOT eligible (requires land ownership in farmer's name)
- KCC: ELIGIBLE with tenant agreement or oral lessee declaration
- PMFBY: ELIGIBLE with sowing certificate from Talathi and cultivator proof
- Gopinath Munde insurance: ELIGIBLE
- Namo Shetkari: NOT eligible without land ownership

Action plan for tenant farmer:
1. Get written tenancy agreement from landowner if possible
2. Form or join JLG (Joint Liability Group) — enables KCC without individual land ownership
3. Enroll in PMFBY as non-loanee farmer with sowing certificate from Talathi
4. Register for Gopinath Munde insurance through Talathi — completely free
5. Approach agriculture extension officer for additional state scheme guidance

GENERAL DISTRESS WARNING SIGNS — SYSTEM SHOULD FLAG THESE:
- PM-KISAN not received in more than 4 months → registration or linkage problem
- Farmer has KCC but does not know if PMFBY is active → verify immediately with bank
- Crop failed but insurance company not informed within 72 hours → claim at risk
- Farmer repaying KCC using informal moneylender loan → dangerous debt cycle
- KCC not renewed in 3+ years → account may be dormant or NPA
- Farmer unaware of Namo Shetkari → potential Rs. 12,000/year unclaimed [VERIFY]
- Farmer in Vidarbha or Marathwada with cotton crop and no insurance → CRITICAL flag
