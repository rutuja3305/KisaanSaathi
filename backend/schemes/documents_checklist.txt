SCHEME NAME: Documents Checklist for Maharashtra Farmer Schemes
SOURCE: Compiled from pmkisan.gov.in, pmfby.gov.in, nabard.org, krishi.maharashtra.gov.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

OBJECTIVE:
Definitive checklist of documents required for each scheme and process. Farmers should keep these documents ready to avoid delays and rejection.

CORE DOCUMENTS EVERY FARMER MUST HAVE (keep originals + 4 photocopies):

7/12 Utara (Satbara Utara):
- What it is: official land ownership and crop record document in Maharashtra
- Issued by: Talathi office in your village/taluka
- Where to get: Talathi office OR online at bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in
- Must be: updated with your current name, current crop sown, and correct land area
- Must NOT be: in deceased parent's name (file Ferfar/mutation first)
- Cost: nominal fee online [VERIFY current fee]
- Validity: get fresh copy within 3 months for bank or scheme applications

Aadhaar Card:
- Mandatory for all central and state government schemes
- Must be: linked to your active bank account (DBT requirement)
- Must be: linked to mobile number for OTP
- If not linked to bank: visit bank branch with original Aadhaar
- If mobile not linked: visit Aadhaar enrollment centre or post office

Bank Passbook (first page):
- Must show: account holder name matching Aadhaar exactly
- Must show: IFSC code and account number clearly
- Must be: active account (used in last 6 months)
- Account must be: in farmer's name (not spouse's or child's for most schemes)
- Account must be: Aadhaar-seeded (linked)

Ration Card:
- For family composition proof in some schemes
- Green/Saffron/White ration card accepted
- Alternative: family declaration certificate from Gram Panchayat

Passport Size Photographs:
- Keep minimum 6 recent photographs ready
- Used for: KCC application, scheme registration, CSC processes

DOCUMENTS BY SCHEME:

PM-KISAN REGISTRATION:
Required:
- Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- 7/12 Utara (must be in applicant's name)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-seeded account)
- Mobile number (for OTP and status updates)
Optional but helpful:
- Ration card (for family proof if name issues)
Common rejection documents to fix first:
- 7/12 with deceased parent's name: file Ferfar at Talathi
- Aadhaar with old address: update at Aadhaar centre if causing mismatch

NAMO SHETKARI REGISTRATION:
- Same documents as PM-KISAN
- PM-KISAN registration must be complete first
- Additional: PM-KISAN registration number or beneficiary status printout

PMFBY ENROLLMENT (NON-LOANEE FARMER):
Required:
- Aadhaar card
- 7/12 Utara showing crop sown this season
- Bank passbook
- Sowing certificate (if required by insurance company for your crop/district) [VERIFY]
- Crop name and area sown (must match 7/12)
For tenant farmer / sharecropper:
- Written tenancy agreement OR
- Sowing certificate from Talathi OR
- Declaration form provided by insurance company

PMFBY CLAIM (after crop loss):
Required:
- Crop loss report acknowledgment number (from 72-hour reporting)
- 7/12 Utara
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook
- Crop failure certificate from Tahsildar (for post-harvest or total loss)
- Photographs of crop damage with date (take these immediately after loss)
- FIR copy if applicable (hailstorm damage with police report)

KCC APPLICATION:
Required:
- Filled application form (from bank, available free)
- Aadhaar card (identity + address proof)
- 7/12 Utara (land ownership proof)
- Passport size photographs (2)
- Bank account details (if existing account at different bank)
For new bank account:
- Open savings account first, then apply for KCC
For tenant farmer:
- Written lease agreement OR joint declaration with landowner
- JLG formation documents if applying as group

KCC LOAN RESTRUCTURING:
Required:
- Crop failure certificate from Tahsildar (most important document)
- KCC account statement (get from bank)
- 7/12 Utara showing crop sown
- Aadhaar card
- Written application for restructuring (write yourself or get help at CSC)
- Drought declaration notification copy (if district declared drought)

GOPINATH MUNDE ACCIDENTAL DEATH CLAIM:
Required:
- Death certificate from Municipal Council or Gram Panchayat
- Post mortem report from government hospital (mandatory)
- FIR copy from police station (for road accident, drowning, assault)
- 7/12 Utara (showing farmer status of deceased)
- Aadhaar card of deceased
- Aadhaar card of nominee/claimant
- Bank account details of nominee (Aadhaar-seeded)
- Proof of relationship: ration card OR birth certificate OR marriage certificate

GOPINATH MUNDE DISABILITY CLAIM:
Required:
- Disability certificate from Civil Surgeon (government hospital) specifying type and percentage
- Hospital discharge summary
- FIR copy if applicable
- 7/12 Utara
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details

DROUGHT COMPENSATION / INPUT SUBSIDY:
No application needed in most cases (automatic)
BUT to ensure your name is included:
- Verify your name in village cultivation records with Talathi before survey
- If name missing: submit written application to Talathi immediately with 7/12 Utara
- Crop failure certificate: get it anyway for bank restructuring

MSP PROCUREMENT REGISTRATION:
Required:
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-seeded)
- 7/12 Utara
- Crop quantity estimate
- Valid mobile number

IMPORTANT DOCUMENT RULES:
Name matching rule:
- Name on Aadhaar, 7/12 Utara, and bank account must match exactly
- Even small spelling differences cause rejection
- Fix mismatches BEFORE applying: Talathi office for 7/12, bank for account name, Aadhaar centre for Aadhaar

7/12 update after family member death:
- File Ferfar (mutation) at Talathi office immediately after death of land-owning family member
- Documents for Ferfar: death certificate, legal heir certificate, existing 7/12, Aadhaar of all heirs
- Takes 30-90 days [VERIFY current processing time]
- Without updated 7/12: cannot register for PM-KISAN, get KCC, or claim PMFBY

Keeping documents safe:
- Store originals in waterproof folder or container (important for Kharif season floods)
- Keep scanned copies on phone or with trusted family member
- DigiLocker (digilocker.gov.in): free government app to store digital copies of all documents

WHERE TO GET DOCUMENTS IF MISSING:
- 7/12 Utara: Talathi office or bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in
- Birth/death certificate: Gram Panchayat or Municipal Council
- Caste certificate: Tahsildar office
- Income certificate: Tahsildar office
- Disability certificate: Government Civil Hospital (Civil Surgeon)
- Legal heir certificate: Tahsildar office
