SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
SOURCE: https://pmfby.gov.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: Kharif 2025 and Rabi 2025-26

---

OBJECTIVE:
Provide financial protection to farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

---

BENEFIT:
- Full crop value insured based on Scale of Finance (SOF) per hectare
- Government pays majority of premium — farmer pays only a small share
- Claim paid directly to bank account after harvest assessment

---

FARMER PREMIUM RATES:
- Kharif crops (Soybean, Cotton, Tur, Maize, Paddy): Maximum 2% of sum insured [VERIFY]
- Rabi crops (Wheat, Gram, Jowar, Safflower): Maximum 1.5% of sum insured [VERIFY]
- Horticulture and annual commercial crops: Maximum 5% of sum insured [VERIFY]
- Remaining premium shared equally by Central and State Government

---

ENROLLMENT DEADLINES:
- Kharif 2025: 31st July 2025 [VERIFY — check pmfby.gov.in for exact date]
- Rabi 2025-26: 31st December 2025 [VERIFY — check pmfby.gov.in for exact date]
- MISSING DEADLINE = NO COVERAGE FOR THAT SEASON — no exceptions

---

LOANEE FARMERS (those with KCC or crop loan):
- PMFBY enrollment is automatic at time of KCC loan disbursement
- Farmer can opt out but must sign written declaration
- Many farmers opt out unknowingly or are opted out by bank staff — VERIFY YOUR ENROLLMENT
- To check: visit your bank branch and ask for PMFBY enrollment confirmation

---

NON-LOANEE FARMERS (no crop loan):
- Enrollment is voluntary — must proactively register before deadline
- Register at: CSC, bank branch, insurance company agent, pmfby.gov.in
- Non-loanee farmers most commonly miss enrollment — highest risk group

---

RISKS COVERED:
- Prevented sowing due to deficit rainfall or adverse weather
- Standing crop loss due to drought, flood, hailstorm, cyclone, pest, disease
- Post-harvest losses up to 14 days after harvest due to hailstorm, cyclone, unseasonal rain
- Localized calamities: hailstorm, landslide, inundation affecting individual farms

---

RISKS NOT COVERED:
- War, nuclear risk
- Malicious damage by farmer
- Theft or negligence
- Losses due to birds and wild animals (unless specifically notified)

---

HOW TO CLAIM:
- Report crop loss within 72 hours of damage — this is mandatory
- Report via: Crop Insurance App (available on Play Store), call insurance company, inform bank branch, or agriculture department
- Get acknowledgment number after reporting — save it
- District crop cutting experiments determine yield loss
- Claim settled within 2 months [VERIFY] of harvest

---

CLAIM REJECTION REASONS TO AVOID:
- Not reporting within 72 hours → claim rejected
- Mobile number not updated → notification missed
- Bank account not Aadhaar-linked → payment fails
- Enrollment deadline missed → no coverage at all
- Crop not notified in district that season → not covered

---

MAHARASHTRA SPECIFIC:
- AIC (Agriculture Insurance Company) and private insurers handle Maharashtra
- District-wise notified crop list changes every season — verify before sowing
- Cotton in Vidarbha (Yavatmal, Amravati, Wardha): notified Kharif crop — verify annually
- Soybean in Marathwada: notified Kharif crop — verify annually

---

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- Crop failure without PMFBY = no income to repay KCC = loan default risk
- PMFBY claim can directly repay KCC outstanding at bank
- Treat PMFBY enrollment as mandatory even if technically voluntary for non-loanee farmers
- Farmers who opted out of PMFBY at KCC time face highest distress risk

---

HOW TO VERIFY ENROLLMENT:
- pmfby.gov.in → Farmer Corner → Application Status
- Visit bank branch with Aadhaar and ask for PMFBY policy document
- Crop Insurance App → Check Policy
