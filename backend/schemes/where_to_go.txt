SCHEME NAME: Where Farmers Should Go for Each Problem in Maharashtra
SOURCE: Compiled from Maharashtra government office hierarchy and scheme guidelines
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

OBJECTIVE:
Tell farmers exactly which office to visit for each type of problem, what to carry, and what to expect. Eliminates guesswork and wasted trips.

TALATHI OFFICE (Village Level Revenue Officer):
Location: Every village or cluster of villages has a Talathi
What they handle:
- 7/12 Utara (Satbara) issuance and correction
- Ferfar (mutation) after land inheritance or purchase
- Crop failure certificate for loan restructuring
- Sowing certificate for tenant farmers (PMFBY)
- Cultivation certificate for Gopinath Munde insurance
- Land record corrections (name spelling, area, crop details)
- Village cultivation records and Panchname (spot inspection)
Bring: Aadhaar, existing 7/12, application written in Marathi or Hindi
Cost: Nominal stamp duty for some services [VERIFY current rates]
Timings: Government office hours, Monday to Saturday [VERIFY local timings]
Response time: 7/12 same day (if records clear), Ferfar 30-90 days, crop certificate 7-15 days
If Talathi is uncooperative: escalate to Circle Officer (Mantralaya level above Talathi)

TAHSILDAR OFFICE (Taluka Level):
Location: Taluka headquarters (one per taluka)
What they handle:
- Crop failure certificate (official, required for bank restructuring)
- Drought declaration information for your taluka
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate
- Legal heir certificate
- Land dispute escalation from Talathi
- Oversees all Talathi offices in the taluka
Bring: 7/12 Utara, Aadhaar, crop sowing proof, application
Response time: Crop failure certificate 15-30 days [VERIFY]

DISTRICT AGRICULTURE OFFICE (DAO):
Location: District headquarters
What they handle:
- PM-KISAN registration problems (name mismatch, rejection, status issues)
- Namo Shetkari registration escalation
- PMFBY grievances and claim delays
- Gopinath Munde insurance claim escalation
- New scheme information and enrollment drives
- KCC awareness and referral to banks
- Farmer training programs
- Seed and fertilizer subsidy schemes
Bring: All relevant scheme documents, previous correspondence, Aadhaar
Who to meet: District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or Scheme-specific desk officer
Response time: Varies — follow up in writing for faster resolution

KRISHI VIGYAN KENDRA (KVK):
Location: One per district approximately
What they handle:
- Crop advisory and pest management (not financial schemes)
- Soil testing
- Seed quality information
- Farming technology and best practices
- Training farmers on new crop varieties
Useful for: Cotton bollworm issue, soil health card program

COMMON SERVICE CENTRE (CSC / Jan Seva Kendra):
Location: Every Gram Panchayat or nearby village [VERIFY coverage in your area]
What they handle:
- PM-KISAN new registration and correction
- Namo Shetkari registration
- Aadhaar update and correction
- DigiLocker document storage
- Government scheme applications online
- Document scanning and printing
Cost: Nominal service charges (Rs. 20-100 per service) [VERIFY]
Important: CSC operator helps submit application — farmer must verify details before submission

BANK BRANCH (Nearest branch where KCC is held):
What they handle:
- KCC application and renewal
- KCC limit enhancement request
- PMFBY enrollment (for loanee farmers)
- Loan restructuring application submission
- Aadhaar-bank account linking
- PM-KISAN and Namo Shetkari DBT bank details update
Bring for KCC: 7/12, Aadhaar, photographs, No Dues Certificate
Bring for restructuring: Crop failure certificate, KCC statement, Aadhaar
Important tip: Always take written acknowledgment for any application submitted at bank

DISTRICT CENTRAL COOPERATIVE BANK (DCCB):
Location: District and taluka level
Specific role in Maharashtra:
- Primary KCC issuer for farmers in rural areas
- More farmer-friendly than nationalized banks for rural credit
- Works with Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) at village level

PRIMARY AGRICULTURAL CREDIT SOCIETY (PACS):
Location: Village level
What they handle:
- Short-term crop loan (alternative to commercial bank KCC)
- Seed and fertilizer distribution on credit
- Onward referral for government schemes
Useful for: Small farmers who cannot access nationalized banks easily

APMC (AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE MARKET COMMITTEE):
Location: Major market towns in each district
What they handle:
- Sale of produce at open auction
- MSP procurement window when activated
- Market price information daily
- Grading and quality assessment
- Farmer registration for MSP procurement
Bring for MSP sale: Aadhaar, bank passbook, 7/12, crop quantity

DISTRICT SUPPLY OFFICE (DSO):
Location: District headquarters
What they handle:
- Below-MSP selling complaints
- Activation of price support when needed
- CCI and NAFED procurement coordination

COLLECTOR OFFICE (DISTRICT COLLECTORATE):
Location: District headquarters
What they handle:
- Drought declaration and relief coordination
- Escalation when all other offices fail to respond
- Ex-gratia for farmer distress deaths
- MNREGA work allocation escalation
- Final authority for district-level relief
When to approach: When DAO, Tahsildar, and bank all fail to respond within reasonable time
How to approach: Written application addressed to District Collector
Response time: Should acknowledge within 7 days [VERIFY]

BANKING OMBUDSMAN:
Location: Regional offices in Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Aurangabad [VERIFY current offices]
Online: cms.rbi.org.in
What they handle:
- Bank refusing loan restructuring without valid reason
- Bank not responding to farmer's restructuring application within 30 days
- Unfair interest charges during moratorium
- KCC-related bank disputes
Cost: Free
How to use: File online complaint with all correspondence history
Response time: 30 days [VERIFY]

INSURANCE COMPANY DISTRICT OFFICE:
What they handle:
- PMFBY claim follow-up and escalation
- Claim rejection disputes
- Premium correction issues
Find contact: pmfby.gov.in → Contact → Insurance company list for your district

QUICK REFERENCE — PROBLEM TO OFFICE:
7/12 correction or issuance → Talathi
Crop failure certificate → Tahsildar
PM-KISAN not received → CSC first, then DAO
Namo Shetkari not received → DAO
PMFBY enrollment → Bank branch or CSC
PMFBY claim delayed → Insurance company district office, then DAO
KCC application → Bank branch
KCC restructuring → Bank branch with crop failure certificate
Bank refusing restructuring → Banking Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in)
MSP procurement → APMC or District Supply Office
Drought compensation → Talathi (verify records) → Tahsildar
Gopinath Munde claim → Talathi → DAO
Land dispute → Talathi → Circle Officer → Tahsildar
Below-MSP complaint → District Supply Office → Collector
All else fails → District Collector written complaint
