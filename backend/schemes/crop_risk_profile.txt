SCHEME NAME: Maharashtra Crop-wise Risk and Scheme Profile
SOURCE: Compiled from agricoop.nic.in, dfpd.gov.in, Maharashtra Agriculture Department
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: Kharif 2025 / Rabi 2025-26

OBJECTIVE:
Define risk profile, typical input costs, relevant schemes, and distress indicators for each major crop grown in Maharashtra.

KHARIF CROPS:

COTTON:
Growing regions: Vidarbha (Yavatmal, Washim, Akola, Amravati), Marathwada (Beed, Hingoli, Nanded)
Season: June sowing, October-February harvesting (long duration crop)
Input cost per acre: Rs. 35,000 to Rs. 55,000 [VERIFY 2025 prices]
MSP 2025-26: Medium staple Rs. 7,121 per quintal / Long staple Rs. 7,521 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: VERY HIGH
Risk factors:
- Pink bollworm resistance: increases pesticide cost by Rs. 5,000-10,000 per acre
- Price volatile: open market price can fall below MSP
- Late or irregular monsoon: direct yield impact
- High input credit requirement: farmers often exceed KCC limit
Relevant schemes: PMFBY (compulsory for notified areas), KCC, PM-KISAN, Namo Shetkari
PMFBY enrollment deadline Kharif 2025: July 31, 2025 [VERIFY]
Distress indicator: Cotton farmer with more than Rs. 1,50,000 KCC + no PMFBY = CRITICAL risk

SOYBEAN:
Growing regions: Marathwada (Latur, Osmanabad, Aurangabad), Vidarbha (Amravati, Yavatmal)
Season: June-July sowing, October-November harvesting
Input cost per acre: Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 22,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 4,892 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: HIGH
Risk factors:
- Entirely rain-dependent: late monsoon causes major loss
- Yellow mosaic virus: common disease risk
- Price fluctuation: often sells below MSP in good harvest years
Relevant schemes: PMFBY, KCC, PM-KISAN
PMFBY coverage: available in most Marathwada and Vidarbha districts

TUR (ARHAR / PIGEON PEA):
Growing regions: Marathwada, Vidarbha, parts of North Maharashtra
Season: June sowing, January-February harvesting (extra-long duration)
Input cost per acre: Rs. 12,000 to Rs. 18,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 7,550 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM-HIGH
Risk factors:
- Long crop period: exposed to multiple weather events
- Wilt disease: common in Marathwada
- Government procurement below MSP in many districts historically
Relevant schemes: PMFBY, KCC, PM-KISAN

PADDY (RICE):
Growing regions: Konkan (Raigad, Ratnagiri), Vidarbha (Gondia, Bhandara, Nagpur), Western Maharashtra
Season: June-July transplanting, October-November harvest
Input cost per acre: Rs. 18,000 to Rs. 28,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Common grade Rs. 2,300 per quintal / Grade A Rs. 2,320 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM
Risk factors:
- Waterlogging in heavy rain years (Konkan)
- Blast disease: common fungal risk
- Price: MSP procurement available through FCI
Relevant schemes: PMFBY, KCC, PM-KISAN

MAIZE:
Growing regions: Nashik, Ahmednagar, parts of Marathwada
Season: June-July sowing, September-October harvest
Input cost per acre: Rs. 12,000 to Rs. 18,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 2,225 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM
Relevant schemes: PMFBY where notified, KCC, PM-KISAN

ONION (KHARIF):
Growing regions: Nashik, Ahmednagar, Solapur
Season: Kharif (July-October), late Kharif (September-December)
Input cost per acre: Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 35,000 [VERIFY]
Price risk: EXTREME — prices crash from Rs. 20/kg to Rs. 1-2/kg in good harvest years
Risk level: HIGH (price risk dominant over weather risk)
Relevant schemes: PMFBY (limited coverage for onion), KCC
Special note: PMFBY coverage for onion is limited and must be verified district-wise [VERIFY]

RABI CROPS:

WHEAT:
Growing regions: Nashik, Pune, Ahmednagar (limited area in Maharashtra)
Season: November sowing, March-April harvest
Input cost per acre: Rs. 12,000 to Rs. 18,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 2,425 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: LOW-MEDIUM
Relevant schemes: PMFBY Rabi, KCC, PM-KISAN
PMFBY enrollment deadline Rabi 2025-26: December 31, 2025 [VERIFY]

GRAM (CHANA / CHICKPEA):
Growing regions: Marathwada, Vidarbha (Rabi season)
Season: October-November sowing, February-March harvest
Input cost per acre: Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 15,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 5,650 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM
Risk factors: Frost risk in December-January, wilt disease
Relevant schemes: PMFBY Rabi, KCC

RABI JOWAR (SORGHUM):
Growing regions: Solapur, Osmanabad, Latur
Season: October sowing, February-March harvest
Input cost per acre: Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 12,000 [VERIFY]
MSP 2025-26: Rs. 3,421 per quintal [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM
Relevant schemes: PMFBY Rabi where notified, KCC

HORTICULTURE CROPS:

GRAPES:
Growing regions: Nashik, Sangli, Solapur, Pune (Baramati)
Input cost per acre: Rs. 1,00,000 to Rs. 2,00,000 per year [VERIFY — very high]
Risk level: HIGH (but also high returns)
Risk factors: Hailstorm, unseasonal rain, downy mildew disease
PMFBY: available under horticulture category at 5% premium [VERIFY]

ORANGE (NAGPUR MANDARIN):
Growing region: Nagpur, Amravati, Wardha
Input cost per acre: Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 60,000 per year [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM-HIGH
Risk factors: Unseasonal rain during flowering, citrus canker
PMFBY: available where notified [VERIFY]

BANANA:
Growing regions: Jalgaon (largest in India), Solapur, Pune
Input cost per acre: Rs. 80,000 to Rs. 1,20,000 per year [VERIFY]
Risk level: MEDIUM
Risk factors: Cyclone, Panama wilt disease
PMFBY: available where notified [VERIFY]

SUGARCANE:
Growing regions: Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara, Solapur, Pune, Ahmednagar
Season: 12-18 months crop
Input cost per acre: Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 80,000 [VERIFY]
Risk level: LOW-MEDIUM (cooperative mills provide price stability)
Relevant schemes: KCC (higher limit for long-duration crop), PM-KISAN
Note: Sugarcane farmers generally have better financial stability due to cooperative system

RISK MATRIX SUMMARY:
CRITICAL risk crops in Maharashtra 2025: Cotton (Vidarbha/Marathwada)
HIGH risk crops: Soybean, Onion, Tur, Grapes
MEDIUM risk crops: Paddy, Maize, Gram, Rabi Jowar, Orange, Banana
LOWER risk crops: Wheat, Sugarcane (irrigated)
