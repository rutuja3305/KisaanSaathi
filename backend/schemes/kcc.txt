SCHEME NAME: Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
SOURCE: https://nabard.org and https://rbi.org.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

---

OBJECTIVE:
Provide short-term and allied activity credit to farmers at subsidized interest rates through a revolving credit facility.

---

BENEFIT:
- Revolving credit limit based on land holding and crop type
- Interest rate: 7% per annum base rate [VERIFY]
- With timely repayment: additional 3% interest subvention from Government
- Effective interest rate with subvention: 4% per annum [VERIFY]
- Personal accident insurance: Rs. 50,000 [VERIFY] included with KCC
- Covers: crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, allied activities, household needs

---

CREDIT LIMIT CALCULATION:
- Based on Scale of Finance (SOF) x area + post-harvest expenses
- Small farmers (less than 1 hectare): Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1,50,000 [VERIFY]
- Medium farmers (1 to 2 hectares): Rs. 1,50,000 to Rs. 3,00,000 [VERIFY]
- Large farmers (above 2 hectares): Above Rs. 3,00,000 [VERIFY] depending on crop and district SOF
- Limit reviewed and enhanced every year automatically

---

ELIGIBILITY:
- Individual farmers owning cultivable land
- Tenant farmers and oral lessees with valid agreement
- Sharecroppers
- Self-Help Groups (SHGs) of farmers
- Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) — group of 4-10 farmers, no individual land ownership required

---

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Application form from bank
- Identity proof: Aadhaar Card preferred
- Address proof
- Land records: 7/12 Utara in Maharashtra
- Passport size photographs (2)
- No Objection Certificate or No Dues Certificate if applicable

---

REPAYMENT TERMS:
- Short-term crop loan: repay within 12 months (one crop cycle)
- Repayment expected after harvest season
- Failure to repay on time: loses 3% subvention, rate becomes 7%
- Extended non-repayment: account classified as NPA (Non-Performing Asset)

---

NPA CONSEQUENCES — CRITICAL:
- NPA classification: damages credit score permanently
- Future loan applications rejected across all banks
- Bank can initiate legal proceedings to attach land as security
- SARFAESI Act applicable for larger loan amounts
- Farmer must NEVER let KCC become NPA without first exploring restructuring options

---

LOAN RESTRUCTURING WHEN FARMER IS IN DISTRESS:
- If crop failure declared in district: bank must restructure KCC per RBI guidelines
- Repayment period extended by up to 2 years [VERIFY]
- Interest subvention continues during restructuring period
- Farmer must approach bank proactively with crop failure certificate from Tahsildar
- Do not wait for bank to contact you — approach bank first

---

KCC AND PMFBY LINKAGE:
- KCC for notified crops triggers automatic PMFBY enrollment
- Farmer can opt out of PMFBY but must sign written declaration
- Banks must inform farmer about PMFBY at KCC issuance — many do not
- If opted out unknowingly: approach bank to reinstate before enrollment deadline

---

MAHARASHTRA SPECIFIC:
- District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) are primary KCC issuers in rural Maharashtra
- Bank of Maharashtra, SBI, Bank of India also active in rural Maharashtra
- Drought-declared districts: automatic moratorium on KCC repayment [VERIFY with current drought declarations]
- Maharashtra state government provides additional interest subvention on top of central scheme [VERIFY current rate]

---

JOINT LIABILITY GROUP (JLG) FOR TENANT FARMERS:
- 4 to 10 farmers form a group
- No individual land ownership required
- Group collectively responsible for repayment
- KCC up to Rs. 1,60,000 [VERIFY] per member without collateral
- Approach bank with group together — bank forms JLG officially

---

LOAN WAIVER HISTORY MAHARASHTRA:
- Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Shetkari Sanman Yojana 2017: waived loans up to Rs. 1.5 lakh [VERIFY]
- Eligibility for any future waiver: must have active KCC at time of announcement
- Farmers with already-defaulted accounts often miss waivers
- Early restructuring protects waiver eligibility — act before NPA classification

---

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- KCC at 4% vs informal moneylender at 24-36% — massive cost difference
- Farmer taking informal loan to repay KCC is in dangerous debt cycle
- First action for any distressed farmer: approach bank for KCC restructuring
- Do not take new informal loan to repay KCC
