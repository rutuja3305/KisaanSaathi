SCHEME NAME: Crop Loan Restructuring and KCC Distress Relief Guidelines
SOURCE: https://rbi.org.in (RBI Master Circular on Relief Measures for Crop Loan)
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025 RBI Guidelines

OBJECTIVE:
When farmers face crop failure or natural calamity, RBI guidelines mandate banks to restructure crop loans and provide relief rather than declaring accounts NPA immediately.

WHEN IS RESTRUCTURING TRIGGERED:
- Crop loss of 33% or more due to natural calamity in the district [VERIFY threshold]
- District or state government declares drought, flood, or crop failure
- Individual farmer can also apply even without official declaration if crop failure is documented
- Bank must consider restructuring if farmer submits crop failure certificate from Tahsildar

KEY RELIEF MEASURES (RBI mandated):
- Repayment period extended by 2 years beyond original due date [VERIFY current RBI circular]
- Interest subvention continues during restructuring period
- No penalty interest on restructured amount
- Account NOT classified as NPA during restructuring period
- Fresh working capital loan can be sanctioned for next season even during restructuring

MORATORIUM RULES:
- When district declared drought: automatic 1-year moratorium on crop loan repayment [VERIFY]
- During moratorium: no interest penalty, account status unchanged
- After moratorium: repayment schedule redrawn based on farmer's capacity
- Farmer must formally apply for moratorium at bank with crop failure certificate

STEP BY STEP PROCESS FOR FARMER IN DISTRESS:

Step 1: Get Crop Failure Certificate
- Visit Tahsildar office in your taluka
- Submit application with: 7/12 Utara, Aadhaar, crop sowing proof
- Tahsildar conducts field inspection
- Certificate issued within 15-30 days [VERIFY current processing time]
- This certificate is your most important document for all relief measures

Step 2: Approach Bank Immediately
- Go to your bank branch with: crop failure certificate, KCC account details, Aadhaar
- Submit written application for loan restructuring/moratorium
- Bank must acknowledge receipt in writing
- Ask for written acknowledgment with date stamp

Step 3: Follow Up
- Banks are mandated by RBI to respond to restructuring requests within 30 days [VERIFY]
- If bank does not respond: escalate to Bank's Grievance Officer
- Further escalation: Banking Ombudsman (free, available in every district)
- RBI Banking Ombudsman: cms.rbi.org.in [VERIFY current portal]

ELIGIBILITY FOR LOAN WAIVER (when active):
- Must have active KCC at time of waiver announcement
- Defaulted accounts often excluded — early action critical
- Small and marginal farmers (less than 2 hectares) typically given priority
- Proof required: 7/12 Utara, KCC account statement, Aadhaar

JOINT LIABILITY GROUP RESTRUCTURING:
- If JLG member defaults: group collectively responsible
- Other group members should approach bank together for group restructuring
- JLG restructuring: same RBI guidelines apply as individual

COOPERATIVE BANK SPECIFIC:
- District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) in Maharashtra follow state cooperative department rules
- May have different restructuring timelines: check with specific DCCB
- Maharashtra State Cooperative Bank provides refinance during drought declarations

BANKING OMBUDSMAN ESCALATION:
- Use when bank refuses restructuring without valid reason
- File complaint: cms.rbi.org.in (free online)
- Or visit Banking Ombudsman office in your region
- Maharashtra regions: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Aurangabad [VERIFY current offices]
- Response time: 30 days [VERIFY]

CRITICAL TIMING RULE:
- Must approach bank BEFORE account becomes NPA (90 days overdue)
- After NPA: restructuring still possible but much harder and longer process
- After NPA: farmer loses eligibility for most waiver schemes
- Time is critical: do not wait for harvest season to pass before acting

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- Restructured loan + PM-KISAN + Namo Shetkari + drought compensation together can bridge farmer through one bad season
- Goal: survive one bad season without permanent credit damage
- Next season: ensure PMFBY enrollment to prevent repeat situation
