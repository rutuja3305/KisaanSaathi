SCHEME NAME: Gopinath Munde Shetkari Apghat Vima Yojana (Farmer Accident Insurance Scheme)
SOURCE: https://krishi.maharashtra.gov.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

---

OBJECTIVE:
Provide free accidental insurance coverage to Maharashtra farmers and their families to protect against sudden financial loss due to accidents, disability, or death.

---

BENEFIT:
- Accidental death: Rs. 2,00,000 [VERIFY] compensation to family
- Permanent total disability (loss of 2 limbs, 2 eyes, or 1 limb + 1 eye): Rs. 2,00,000 [VERIFY]
- Permanent partial disability (loss of 1 limb or 1 eye): Rs. 1,00,000 [VERIFY]
- Premium fully paid by Maharashtra Government — farmer pays NOTHING
- This scheme is completely free for eligible farmers

---

ELIGIBILITY:
- Age: 10 to 75 years [VERIFY]
- Must be a farmer (name in land records) OR family member of farmer
- Covers: farmer, spouse, and up to 2 earning children
- Both landowner farmers and tenant farmers are eligible

---

ACCIDENTS COVERED:
- Road accident (vehicle collision, fall from vehicle)
- Drowning
- Snake bite
- Lightning strike
- Fall from tree
- Agricultural machinery accident (tractor, thresher, pump)
- Fire accident
- Any other sudden unexpected accident resulting in death or disability

---

ACCIDENTS NOT COVERED:
- Suicide (intentional self-harm is excluded)
- Death due to illness or disease (not an accident)
- Accidents under influence of alcohol
- Self-inflicted injuries

---

HOW TO CLAIM:
- Report to Talathi office within 90 days of accident [VERIFY]
- Talathi verifies and forwards to District Agriculture Officer
- Claim settled within 30 days [VERIFY] of complete documentation submission

---

DOCUMENTS FOR CLAIM:
- Death certificate (if death) from Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat
- Disability certificate from government hospital (if disability)
- FIR copy from police station (if applicable — road accident, drowning)
- Post-mortem report if applicable
- 7/12 Utara showing farmer status
- Aadhaar card of farmer and claimant
- Bank account details of claimant (family member for death claim)
- Relationship proof (for family member claiming after death)

---

IMPORTANT FOR DISTRESS SITUATIONS:
- Many farmer families in Maharashtra are unaware this scheme exists
- In Vidarbha and Marathwada where farmer accidents and distress deaths are high, this scheme provides critical family protection
- Family members of deceased farmer must claim within 90 days — deadline is strict
- Talathi office is the first point of contact — visit immediately after accident

---

MAHARASHTRA SPECIFIC:
- Scheme implemented through Maharashtra Agriculture Department
- District Agriculture Officer is the approving authority
- Insurance company empanelled by Maharashtra government handles actual payout [VERIFY current empanelled insurer]

---

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- Accidental death of farmer leaves family with KCC loan outstanding and no income
- Rs. 2,00,000 compensation can clear small KCC loans entirely
- Reduces secondary financial distress caused by primary accident
- Tenant farmers and sharecroppers also eligible — often unaware of this

---

HELPLINE:
