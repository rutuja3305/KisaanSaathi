OBJECTIVE:
Classify Maharashtra districts by agricultural distress risk level based on historical drought frequency, rainfall patterns, crop dependency, and farmer distress data.

HIGH RISK ZONE — MARATHWADA (8 districts):
Districts: Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Latur, Osmanabad (Dharashiv), Nanded, Beed, Hingoli, Parbhani, Jalna
Risk factors:
- Rainfall: 600 to 800 mm annually, highly irregular [VERIFY]
- Drought declared in 4 out of last 10 years in most districts [VERIFY]
- Primary crops: Cotton, Soybean, Tur — all rain-dependent
- Groundwater depletion: severe in Beed, Osmanabad, Aurangabad
- Farmer distress incidents: highest concentration in Maharashtra [VERIFY NCRB data]
- KCC default rate: above state average [VERIFY]
Specific risk notes:
- Beed: cotton + onion, both price-volatile crops
- Osmanabad: soybean dominant, late monsoon common
- Latur: Rabi sorghum dependent, dam water scarcity
Active support: PMFBY compulsory for notified crops, drought relief when declared

HIGH RISK ZONE — VIDARBHA (11 districts):
Districts: Yavatmal, Washim, Buldhana, Amravati, Wardha, Akola, Nagpur, Chandrapur, Gadchiroli, Gondia, Bhandara
Risk factors:
- Yavatmal, Washim, Buldhana: extreme cotton distress zone
- Cotton input cost highest in state: Rs. 35,000 to Rs. 55,000 per acre [VERIFY]
- Bt cotton pink bollworm resistance increasing since 2018
- Rainfall: 700 to 1100 mm but irregular distribution
- Chandrapur, Gadchiroli: tribal farmer concentration, lower scheme awareness
- Nagpur: orange horticulture belt, separate risk profile
Specific risk notes:
- Yavatmal: highest pesticide-related farmer incidents in India historically [VERIFY]
- Wardha: Gandhian cotton belt, organic farming movement
- Gondia, Bhandara: paddy cultivation, waterlogging risk
Active support: PMFBY, Vasantrao Naik Shetkari Swavalamban Mission active in Vidarbha

MEDIUM RISK ZONE — NORTH MAHARASHTRA (4 districts):
Districts: Nashik, Ahmednagar, Dhule, Jalgaon, Nandurbar
Risk factors:
- Nashik: onion capital — price crash risk even in good harvest year
- Ahmednagar: sugarcane + drought-prone talukas mixed
- Dhule, Nandurbar: tribal areas, low irrigation coverage
- Jalgaon: banana belt, irrigation dependent
Specific risk notes:
- Onion price crash in Nashik: farmer gets Rs. 1-2 per kg when wholesale crashes [VERIFY current prices]
- Nandurbar: adivasi farmer zone, special tribal schemes applicable

MEDIUM RISK ZONE — PUNE DIVISION (5 districts):
Districts: Pune, Solapur, Satara, Sangli, Kolhapur
Risk factors:
- Solapur: drought-prone eastern talukas, moderate risk
- Satara: mixed irrigation, moderate stability
- Sangli: sugarcane dominant, better irrigation
- Kolhapur: highest irrigation coverage in Maharashtra, lower risk
- Pune: mixed — eastern talukas drought-prone, western talukas better
Specific risk notes:
- Solapur: grape and pomegranate in some areas, horticulture insurance important
- Kolhapur: cooperative sugar mills strong, relatively stable farmer income

LOW RISK ZONE — KONKAN (5 districts):
Districts: Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg, Raigad, Thane (rural), Palghar
Risk factors:
- Highest rainfall in Maharashtra: 2500 to 4000 mm annually [VERIFY]
- Primary crops: Rice (paddy), Coconut, Mango, Cashew
- Smaller land holdings but stable income
- Main risk: post-harvest losses, price for mango and cashew
Specific risk notes:
- Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg: Alphonso mango belt, horticulture PMFBY important
- Paddy cultivation: waterlogging risk in heavy rain years
