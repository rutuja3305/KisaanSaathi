SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
SOURCE: https://pmkisan.gov.in
LAST VERIFIED: June 2025
VERSION: 2025-26

---

OBJECTIVE:
Provide direct income support to farmer families to meet agricultural input costs and household needs.

---

BENEFIT:
- Rs. 6000 [VERIFY] per year paid directly to farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Paid in 3 installments of Rs. 2000 [VERIFY] each
- Installment schedule: April-July, August-November, December-March
- Payment via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) only

---

ELIGIBILITY:
- All landholding farmer families with cultivable land registered in their name
- Applicable to small, marginal, and large landholders
- Family definition: husband, wife, and minor children below 18 years
- Land must be in farmer's name OR spouse's name in official land records

---

WHO IS NOT ELIGIBLE:
- Institutional landholders (trusts, companies)
- Farmer families where any member paid income tax in last assessment year
- Former or current holders of constitutional posts
- Government servants drawing regular salary — except Multi-Tasking Staff, Class IV, Group D employees
- Retired pensioners with monthly pension above Rs. 10,000 [VERIFY]
- Professionals: doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, architects

---

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Land record: 7/12 Utara (Satbara) in Maharashtra
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Active mobile number

---

MAHARASHTRA SPECIFIC:
- 7/12 Utara (Satbara) is the mandatory land record document
- Talathi office handles land record verification and corrections
- Name on 7/12 must exactly match Aadhaar name — mismatch causes rejection
- Mutation (Ferfar) must be done if land was inherited — untransferred land causes failure

---

HOW TO APPLY:
- Online: pmkisan.gov.in → Farmer Corner → New Farmer Registration
- Common Service Centre (CSC)
- Talathi office in Maharashtra
- Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)

---

HOW TO CHECK INSTALLMENT STATUS:
- pmkisan.gov.in → Farmer Corner → Beneficiary Status → enter Aadhaar or mobile
- If installment not received in 4+ months: check Aadhaar-bank linkage at bank

---

COMMON REASONS FOR REJECTION OR MISSING INSTALLMENTS:
- Aadhaar not linked to bank account → visit bank with Aadhaar to link
- Name mismatch between Aadhaar and 7/12 → correction at Talathi office
- Land mutation not done after inheritance → update Ferfar at Talathi
- Duplicate registration → visit CSC with Aadhaar to resolve
- Bank account inactive or frozen → activate at bank branch
- Mobile number not registered → update at CSC or bank

---

DISTRESS LINKAGE:
- Farmers in loan distress must first check all pending PM-KISAN installments
- Each missed installment = Rs. 2000 unclaimed
- If missed for 2 years = Rs. 12,000 potentially recoverable
- Recovering pending installments can offset short-term loan repayment pressure
